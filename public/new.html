Gayzelles Sporting Club

We are a free LGBT+ sporting club based in Southeast London
Abbey Wood, Thamesmead and Woolwich

We host weekly local events. Everyone is friendly and everyone is welcomed.

Running
Where it all began. Our friendly Tuesday evening run loops around Woolwich and Thamesmead. We run together with different pace groups. Our Saturday run is at the Woolwich Thamespath Parkrun. It is the closest parkrun for most of our members. Contact our Running Leads to join one of our runs!
